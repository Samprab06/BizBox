Prepared at build time with identifying User-Agent `BizBox-AssetPreparation/1.0`. The live API is not required at application runtime. Retain the in-app “Furniture models by Poly Haven · CC0” credit.

Model dimensions describe the render mesh, not a manufacturer's product specification, permitted occupancy, accessibility clearance, price, or equipment throughput. BizBox-authored seat counts and operational bindings are separate modeling assumptions.
