What do these results depend on?

This example uses 110 generated customer arrivals and the same service requirements for both plans. Its outcomes depend on demand, service time, staffing, and included costs. Business comparisons should expose their inputs, sources, and assumptions. These are model results, not a forecast for the public Matterport café.
